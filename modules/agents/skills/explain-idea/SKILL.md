---
name: explain-idea
description: Turn an idea interview into a static site with a narrated video and an interactive explainer.
---

1. **Interview.** Group [grill-me](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/main/skills/productivity/grill-me/SKILL.md) questions to clarify audience, takeaway, and flow. Ask which style they want. Offer [Vox-style maps and paper collage](https://github.com/prakhar2603/collage-skill), [Peter Yang's Motion, Illustrated, or 3D](https://github.com/petergyang/video-explainer), [kinetic type](https://github.com/immamdouhaboammar/motion-graphics-skills), [3D object explainer](https://github.com/vargHQ/skills/blob/main/code-3d-explainer/SKILL.md), or their own.
2. **Agree on the beats.** Use pstack's [teach](https://github.com/backnotprop/pstack/blob/main/skills/teach/SKILL.md) and local `plain-writing`. Agree on shared beats for both formats and a short sourced fact sheet before any video.
3. **Explore.** Use only HyperFrames and three.js for video production. Always make two clearly different playable compositions from the same brief, one in each.
   - Use local Kokoro for quick development and exploration. Where the style suits word-level sync, use faster-whisper narration timestamps to cue reveals at word ± frames. Where sound effects fit, synthesize cue-timed paper slaps, marker strokes, typing, or whooshes.
   - **HyperFrames.** Use the official [`/hyperframes`](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/blob/main/skills/hyperframes/SKILL.md), [`/hyperframes-cli`](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/blob/main/skills/hyperframes-cli/SKILL.md), and [`/media-use`](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/blob/main/skills/media-use/SKILL.md) skills. See [`references/types/explainer.md`](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/blob/main/skills/hyperframes/references/types/explainer.md) and [`3d.md`](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/blob/main/skills/hyperframes/references/types/3d.md). If parallel capture hangs, render with `--workers 1`. Prefer a real GPU.
   - **three.js.** Follow vargHQ's `code-3d-explainer`. Keep `render(t)` a pure function of time, seed the RNG, vendor three.js, and use HTML labels.
   - **HTML.** Develop the interactive page in parallel. Let readers change inputs and see their effects. Consult [visual-explainer](https://github.com/nicobailon/visual-explainer) and [Vizli](https://github.com/weisberg/agent_tools/blob/main/tools/vizli/skills/explainer/SKILL.md). See [Explorable Explanations](https://worrydream.com/ExplorableExplanations/) and [Parable of the Polygons](https://ncase.me/polygons/).
4. **Compare.** Review each video with a contact sheet of stills. Show both, explain what each does better, and let the user pick. Adapt the pick using the best of both. Three.js inside HyperFrames is fine.
5. **Produce.** Ask whether to upgrade to VoxCPM2 or Qwen3-TTS for emotion or a specific narrator. If using word-level sync, re-sync to the final narration. Deliver one static site with the interactive explainer at `/` and the narrated composition at `/video/` with play, pause, and seek controls. Link the two pages.

Read upstream instructions and supporting files only when the current step needs them.
